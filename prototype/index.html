Bim: Grow the Island

Grow food for Barbados. Feed 6 of the 11 parishes and reach the Co-op Age before AgroCorp buys up the island.

  • Tap a worker, then tap breadfruit, coral stone, a spring or flying fish to gather.
  • Build chattel houses, farm plots, cisterns and seawalls in your parishes.
  • Tap a parish next to yours to feed it and win it over.
  • Hurricanes hit every few minutes. Seawalls shield nearby buildings.
  • Drag to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
