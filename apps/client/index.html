The Recovery Age

The cane years

From the 1640s, most of Barbados was cleared for sugar cane. The work was done by enslaved Africans, and after full emancipation in 1838 by their free descendants, often for very little pay.

Three centuries of one crop wore out the soil and left the island buying much of its food from abroad.

This age is about repairing that land: compost to rebuild the soil, windbreaks to hold it, and many crops instead of one.

Bim: Grow the Island

Grow food for Barbados. Feed 6 of the 11 parishes and grow through five ages to the Co-op Age before AgroCorp buys up the island.

  • Tap a worker, then tap breadfruit, coral stone, a spring or flying fish to gather.
  • Build chattel houses, farm plots, cisterns and seawalls in your parishes.
  • Tap a parish next to yours to feed it and win it over.
  • Money (BBD) comes from co-op dues in every parish you feed, and from selling food at your Town Centre.
  • Hurricanes hit every few minutes. Seawalls shield nearby buildings.
